Company Story

A Decade of Pest Management in Indore

Malwa-Tec Services has been active in the pest control business for 10+ years, working with both residential customers and commercial properties across Indore, Ujjain, Dewas, Rau and Mhow.

The business is led by owner Shoeb Amin, who brings 15+ years of experience in pest control services. Over the years, Malwa-Tec Services has grown to serve 1,600+ residential customers and 1,100+ commercial customers — 2,700+ customers in total.

Our approach centres on understanding each property's pest concern before planning a visit, so that homes, offices, factories, warehouses, restaurants, hotels, hospitals, shops and institutions all receive service suited to how they actually operate.
