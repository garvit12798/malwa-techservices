1. Introduction

Malwa-Tec Services ("we", "us", "our") operates this website to provide information about our residential and commercial pest control services in Indore, Ujjain, Dewas, Rau and Mhow. This Privacy Policy explains how information submitted through this website may be collected, used and protected.

2. Information We May Collect

We may collect information that visitors choose to provide, such as name, phone number, email address, property type, service required, and details of a pest control enquiry. We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information beyond what is needed to respond to an enquiry.

3. Contact Form Information

When you submit the enquiry form on our Contact page, the information you provide (such as your name, phone number, service required and message) is sent to Malwa-Tec Services in order to respond to your request. This may be processed through a third-party form-handling service configured for this website.

4. How Information May Be Used

Information submitted through this website may be used to respond to enquiries, schedule services, and communicate with you about your requirement. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

5. Communication

We may contact you by phone, WhatsApp, or email using the details you provide, in relation to your enquiry.

6. Cookies and Analytics

This website may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality and, if enabled, analytics tools to understand website usage. If analytics tools such as Google Analytics are added to this site, this section should be updated to reflect that.

7. Third-Party Services

This website may link to third-party services such as WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, and form-handling providers. These third parties have their own privacy practices, which we encourage you to review.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through this website, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We retain enquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and for legitimate business record-keeping.

10. User Rights

You may contact us to ask what information we hold about you, or to request that it be corrected or deleted, subject to applicable law.
